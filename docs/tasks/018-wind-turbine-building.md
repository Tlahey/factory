# Feature: Wind turbine building

**Status:** Not started

## Description

A new building that produces energy based on wind speed, most effective when placed in windier zones.

## Requirements

- Produces energy based on wind speed.
- Should be placed in zones with stronger wind to maximize output.
- Blade-rotation animation whose speed varies with wind speed.
- UI shows real-time energy output.
- Skill-tree upgrades increase output and resistance to extreme weather conditions.
